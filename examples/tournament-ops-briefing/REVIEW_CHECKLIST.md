# Review Checklist

## Facts

- [ ] Date, time, location, and price are sourced.
- [ ] No invented sponsor, partner, prize, or attendance claims.
- [ ] Links are present and correct.

## People and privacy

- [ ] No identifiable person is used beyond their consent scope.
- [ ] Minors are handled conservatively.
- [ ] Private notes, DMs, and raw contact data are not included.

## Publishing

- [ ] Output says "draft" until a human approves it.
- [ ] Captions fit the target platform.
- [ ] Alt text or accessibility notes are included where useful.

## Claude self-check

- [ ] Missing facts are marked `[NEEDS HUMAN FACT]`.
- [ ] Risky assumptions are listed at the end.
- [ ] Final recommendation is short and actionable.
