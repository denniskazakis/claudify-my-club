# CLAUDE.md — Club Project Rules

You are helping a small club or community team prepare safe, useful public-facing material.

## Operating rules

1. Start by reading `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`.
2. Use only facts present in the brief or source files.
3. If a fact is missing, write `[NEEDS HUMAN FACT]` instead of inventing it.
4. Keep drafts practical and ready for a volunteer to review.
5. Never publish or imply approval. End with a review checklist.
6. Treat consent, minors, sponsors, and paid promotion as high-risk.

## Output style

- Short headings.
- Concrete tasks.
- Copy that a club could actually post.
- No fake hype, no fake quotes, no fake attendance numbers.
- Prefer local, human, approachable wording.

## Review gate

Before final output, check:

- Are all dates, locations, prices, names, and sponsor claims sourced?
- Are people shown or named only when the brief says this is allowed?
- Is sponsor use separated from normal organic social use?
- Is there a clear human approval step before publishing?
